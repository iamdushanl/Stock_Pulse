Haycarb posts Rs. 40.6 bn 1H revenue, profit before tax up 75% to Rs. 4.0 bn
Haycarb PLC, a member of the Hayleys Group, delivered a strong financial performance for the
six months ending 30th September 2026, recording revenue of Rs. 40.6 billion, up 42% year-on-
year, while profit before tax increased 75% to Rs. 4.0 billion. Attributable Profit to shareholders
grew 98% to Rs. 2.5 billion, with earnings per share increasing to Rs. 8.41.
The Group strengthened its position in gold recovery and water treatment applications through
increased market penetration and product development initiatives, diversifying its revenue base
and supporting margin enhancement. A key contributor to growth during the period was the
continued expansion of Haycarb's energy storage carbon portfolio, where global demand for
specialized activated carbons used in advanced energy storage applications remains robust.
Mohan Pandithage, Chairman of Haycarb PLC, and its parent company Hayleys PLC said: “The
Group's performance reflects the continued successful execution of its transformation strategy,
focused on expanding its portfolio of value-added activated carbon solutions, market growth,
strengthening its global manufacturing footprint, and enhancing resilience across its supply chain
within the ESG framework.”
The period was also characterised by significant volatility in coconut shell charcoal availability
and pricing across several sourcing markets. Amid these challenges, Haycarb's established
sourcing and manufacturing ecosystem of extensive supplier network, diversified raw material
sourcing model, and geographically distributed manufacturing operations served as a strategic
competitive advantage and enabled the Group to maintain continuity of supply and support
customer requirements across key global markets.
To support growing demand and mitigate supply-side risks amid geopolitical tensions and extreme
climate impacts, Haycarb strategically increased raw material and finished goods inventory
holdings during the period which enabled the Group to maintain production stability and customer
service levels. Trade receivables also increased in line with business growth, reflecting the higher
revenue base achieved during the period.
Rajitha Kariyawasan, Managing Director, Haycarb PLC said: “The Company continued to
advance several strategic growth initiatives during the period as part of its long-term value creation
agenda. Capital expenditure amounted to Rs. 4.8 billion at Group level during the six months under
review, of which Rs. 3.0 billion was invested by the Sri Lankan operations. These investments
mainly support ongoing capacity expansion of higher-value activated carbon products for energy
storage applications in Sri Lanka and deployment of the strategic initiative of a greenfield
manufacturing location in Philippines.”
The increase in capital employed is aligned with the Group's ongoing capital expansion programme
and commitment to building long-term capacity and market leadership in value added product
segments, supply chain resilience, and future earnings potential.
Looking ahead, Haycarb remains well positioned to capitalise on growing global demand for
sustainable purification, gold recovery, and energy storage solutions. Backed by its global
manufacturing footprint, strong sourcing network and continued investment in sustainable
innovation and value addition, the Group remains confident in its ability to deliver long-term
growth while advancing its transformation into a leading provider of specialised activated carbon
solutions worldwide.
Haycarb is the pioneer manufacturer of coconut shell activated carbon in any coconut producing
country with manufacturing facilities in Sri Lanka, Thailand and Indonesia supported by marketing
offices in the USA, UK, Germany, and Australia. The company contributes net foreign exchange
revenues with its value-adding processes and is a leading and technologically superior
manufacturer in its chosen segment.
