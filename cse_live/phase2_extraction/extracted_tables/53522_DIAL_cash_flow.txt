Loss of Control in Digital Health (Private) Limited
In October 2026, the Group sold a 20% interest in Digital Health (Private) Limited, comprising 16.65% to Sunshine Healthcare
Lanka Limited for LKR 312.2 million and 3.35% to Nawaloka Hospitals PLC for LKR 62.7 million. Following the transaction,
s effective shareholding reduced from 64.05% to 44.05%, resulting in Digital Health (Private) Limited ceasing to be
a subsidiary and being accounted for as an associate. In accordance with LKAS 10 Events after the Reporting Period, this
is considered a non-adjusting event and, accordingly, no adjustment has been made to the financial statements as at the
reporting date.
Interim Dividend for the financial year 2026
The Board of Directors of the Company has proposed an interim dividend of LKR 0.70 per share for the financial year 2026.
As required by section 56 (2) of the Companies Act No. 07 of 2007, the Board of Directors had confirmed that the Company
satisfied the solvency test in accordance with section 57 of the Companies Act No. 07 of 2007, and obtained a certificate from
auditors, prior to declaring the dividend.
